# Amiga

## Why did DOS take off but not Amiga? | /r/retrogaming

https://www.reddit.com/r/retrogaming/comments/r483k9/why_did_dos_take_off_but_not_amiga/

Sirotaca: If you want a serious answer, the Amiga was doomed in the US by its lack of interoperability with the IBM PC and its clones, which were already rapidly becoming the industry standard over here. When it was first introduced, it cost as much as a nice PC clone (the Tandy 1000 was around the same price), but frankly wasn't as useful due to the lack of software and so forth. Those issues were solved over time, but the first impression wasn't great. So their only real audiences initially were digital artists, which was a very new idea at the time and wasn't a big market, and gamers who mostly went for the far cheaper NES instead.
